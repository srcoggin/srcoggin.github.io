The Las Vegas Raiders are adding a familiar Bay Area name to their staff, reportedly hiring Omar Young as their running backs coach, a move that underscores the franchise’s ongoing effort to reshape its coaching infrastructure while tightening its connection to its roots.

Yahoo Sports NFL reported the hire and noted Young is an Oakland native, bringing a hometown link to the Silver and Black — a franchise that spent decades in Oakland before relocating to Las Vegas. While the Raiders have not detailed the scope of Young’s responsibilities in the report, the running backs coach role carries direct influence over one of the most foundational parts of an offense: the run game’s weekly preparation, player development and in-game usage, from pass protection to ball security and situational packages.

The addition matters for a Raiders team that has made coaching continuity and player development central talking points in recent seasons, especially as the league continues to place a premium on efficient run-pass balance and backfield versatility. A running backs coach is often a key conduit between the coordinator, the offensive line and the backs themselves, translating scheme into technique and consistency — details that can swing close games across a long season.

Young’s reported arrival also carries symbolic weight. The Raiders’ identity has long been intertwined with Oakland, even after moves that took the team to Los Angeles and later to Las Vegas. Hiring an Oakland native offers a reminder of that lineage as the organization continues to build its on-field product in its newest home market.
