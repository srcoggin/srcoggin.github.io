The San Francisco 49ers’ 2025 travel ledger is gaining another major stamp, with the franchise set to serve as the home team for a regular-season game in Mexico City in December. While the league has not announced the opponent or exact date in the source material, the designation of San Francisco as the home team is significant: it means the 49ers will surrender a game at Levi’s Stadium in exchange for the international showcase, a tradeoff teams accept for the marketing reach and the league’s growing overseas footprint.
