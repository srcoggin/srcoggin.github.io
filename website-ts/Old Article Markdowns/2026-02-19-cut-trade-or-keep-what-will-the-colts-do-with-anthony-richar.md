Indianapolis’ offseason quarterback plan has taken an unexpected turn, leaving the Colts facing a high-stakes decision on one of the franchise’s biggest recent investments. After Anthony Richardson, the 2023 No. 4 overall pick, fell behind Riley Leonard on the depth chart, Indianapolis must determine whether to keep developing him, explore a trade market, or move on entirely, ESPN reported.

The shift carries franchise-level implications because Richardson was drafted to be a cornerstone. A quarterback taken that high is typically afforded years — and a clear runway — to grow into the job. Falling behind Leonard changes the calculus, not just for the Colts’ immediate lineup but for how the organization allocates practice reps, roster spots and future resources at the most important position in the sport.

ESPN framed the decision in blunt terms: cut, trade or keep. Each path would represent a dramatically different signal about where Richardson stands inside the building. Keeping him would suggest Indianapolis still believes his long-term upside merits patience, even if Leonard is ahead right now. Trading him would indicate the Colts may be ready to recoup value — or at least clear the deck — while another team gambles on Richardson’s pedigree as a top-five selection. Cutting him would be the most definitive outcome, a rare and stark acknowledgment that the partnership did not develop as planned.

For fans, the storyline matters because quarterback clarity shapes everything — from how an offense is built to how a team navigates the draft and free agency. Indianapolis’ depth chart movement also invites broader questions about how quickly teams are willing to pivot in today’s NFL, where performance timelines are compressed and roster decisions are increasingly transactional.

Richardson’s status also affects the rest of the quarterback room. Leonard’s rise into a leading position on the depth chart creates a new focal point for the Colts’ evaluation process, while Richardson’s next steps could determine whether Indianapolis carries a high-profile backup project, shops him to quarterback-needy clubs, or considers more drastic measures.
