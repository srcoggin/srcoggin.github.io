The Miami Dolphins have released wide receiver Tyreek Hill, a franchise-altering move that closes the book on one of the NFL’s most explosive playmakers and signals a major shift in the team’s offensive future. The decision comes with Hill still dealing with the aftermath of a knee injury that sidelined him for most of the 2025 season, and it arrives before contract guarantees scheduled for 2026, according to CBS Sports NFL.

Hill’s departure is significant on multiple fronts. On the field, he has been the centerpiece of Miami’s passing attack since arriving as a transformational talent, the type of player who can tilt defensive game plans and change games with a single touch. Off the field, the timing underscores the cold reality of NFL roster management: availability and contract structure often drive personnel decisions as much as past production.

CBS Sports NFL reported Miami moved on from Hill “ahead of 2026 contract guarantees,” framing the release as a proactive decision tied to the financial calendar as much as the injury. Hill’s knee issue dominated his 2025 campaign, with the receiver missing most of the season. For a team built around speed and explosive plays, losing Hill for extended time created a ripple effect — changing not only how defenses played Miami, but also how Miami could call games week to week without its most feared weapon consistently available.

Moving on from that caliber of player usually indicates either an unavoidable health concern, a major cap-related decision, or both.
