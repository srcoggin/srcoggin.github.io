The move is the latest ripple in a reshaping process for a franchise that has spent the early part of the offseason recalibrating its staff and structure.

Williams’ exit underscores the volatility that often follows a season’s end, particularly for teams adjusting their coaching pipeline. For Philadelphia, the change adds another layer of transition on a staff that has been in motion as the offseason progresses.
